## 1. Formation, hierarchy and electronic evidence

The contract is formed by the accepted proposal/order, this version of the terms, the immutable offer/scope snapshot and any signed addendum. In a conflict, the signed addendum prevails, followed by the order, scope snapshot and these terms. Electronic acceptance must preserve CNPJ, authorized representative, timestamp, IP/device evidence when available, document hash/version and a downloadable copy. No checkout, callback or provider object alone proves acceptance, payment or service activation.

## 2. Object, professional boundaries and client duties

CONFENGE provides the advisory, intelligence, coordination and deliverables expressly listed in the accepted offer. The service is an obligation of means, not a promise of award, contract, approval, revenue, savings or administrative outcome. It does not include legal representation/opinion, filing, signature, advocacy, influence, public-agent contact on the client's behalf, execution of engineering works, ART/RRT, physical surveys/tests, guarantees or any excluded item unless a separately approved addendum says otherwise.

The client must provide lawful, accurate and timely data, access, decisions and authorized contacts; validate technical, legal, tax, pricing and filing decisions; and remain responsible for signatures, portals, certificates, proposals and performance of its public contracts. Delays, omissions or changes attributable to the client suspend affected deadlines without placing CONFENGE in default.

## 3. Price, taxes, invoice and payment-provider fees

Public prices are in BRL, gross and inclusive of the ordinary payment-provider fee and the taxes assumed in the approved commercial model. The current pricing premise is **6% under Simples Nacional**, but this is not a representation that 6% is the company's legally effective tax rate: engineering/consulting revenue may fall under Annex III or V according to the factor R, RBT12, activity, municipality, service code, withholding and then-current law. Finance must use the legally correct NFS-e classification and effective rate validated by the accountant. A tax or fee change does not silently alter an existing contract; it requires a new offer/terms version or signed addendum, without retroactivity.

Production NFS-e remains blocked until accountant validation.

## 4. Late payment, notices, suspension and collection

An overdue amount incurs, from the day after maturity: **(a) one-time late-payment penalty of 2% over the overdue installment; (b) simple interest of 1% per month, calculated pro rata die; and (c) IPCA monetary adjustment, without compounding of the contractual interest**. Payments apply first to documented costs legally recoverable, then adjustment, interest, penalty and principal. CONFENGE sends an overdue notice and may suspend unperformed services after 10 calendar days of delay, with at least two business days' written warning, preserving data and an opportunity to cure. It may terminate for cause after 30 calendar days overdue. Undisputed amounts remain payable. Collection costs are limited to documented, legally recoverable costs and fees awarded or agreed in a specific settlement; no automatic extrajudicial attorney-fee percentage is created by this version.

## 5. Capacity and service clocks

Recurring Full service has a global hard cap of **50 active slots**, with one standard contract consuming one slot unless an addendum states more. Capacity approval creates a provisional 72-hour hold tied to CNPJ, offer and start window; final reservation occurs only after confirmed first payment. Expired/failed checkout releases the hold. No capacity means no recurring checkout: the lead may enter a waitlist, contract a fit conversation or use an eligible one-off service.

Standardized one-off services have **no fixed commercial slot cap**, but are never an unlimited concurrent-performance guarantee. Each request passes legality, completeness, conflict and feasibility acceptance before checkout. For an accepted standardized one-off, the delivery clock is **48 business hours** after complete brief/data and written acceptance; client delay, force majeure or approved scope change pauses/restarts the affected clock. `CFG-DIAG-EXP-v1` is expressly excluded from that clock and retains its 10–15-business-day delivery term.

## 6. Activation, acceptance and rework

Kickoff/onboarding begins only after confirmed payment, accepted terms and, where applicable, final capacity reservation. A deliverable is deemed operationally received on availability at the agreed channel. The client has five business days to report an objective nonconformity against the written scope; CONFENGE will correct a confirmed nonconformity within a reasonable agreed period. Silence is operational acceptance, without waiving latent defects or mandatory rights. New facts, preferences or scope are change requests, not rework.

The first confirmed charge must precede kickoff/activation.

## 7. Cancellation, fixed commitments and renewal

Flex may be terminated on 30 days' written, billable notice. The service and charges continue through the effective date; an informal message does not cancel the provider subscription or waive balance reconciliation.

For 180 and 365, early convenience termination becomes effective after 30 days' notice. Amounts due through that date remain payable, plus discount recomposition equal to the plan's monthly discount against Flex multiplied by the service months started: **R$ 5,000 × months for 180** and **R$ 7,500 × months for 365**. Recomposition is capped at the lowest of **(i) the calculated accrued discount, (ii) 20% of the original total commitment, and (iii) the nominal unpaid contract balance**. It is reduced proportionally where required by law and is waived when termination results from uncured material breach by CONFENGE. The 180/365 plans end at `maxPayments`; there is no silent renewal. Continuation requires a newly accepted version/order.

## 8. Refund, duplicate charge and chargeback

CONFENGE refunds an undue or duplicate charge after reconciliation. If CONFENGE refuses an accepted service before starting, the paid amount is refunded in full. After work starts, a client convenience cancellation is settled against work demonstrably performed, committed capacity and the applicable cancellation clause, with any positive balance refunded. A chargeback opens an exception case and may suspend unperformed work until reconciliation; it does not by itself erase a valid debt. Every real refund, cancellation or provider mutation remains a single-action finance approval with audit evidence.

## 9. Confidentiality, data protection and security

Each party protects non-public commercial, technical, personal and public-procurement information, using it only to perform the contract and disclose it only to personnel/providers under compatible duties or when legally required. Personal data is processed under the LGPD on an appropriate legal basis, with minimization, access control, retention/deletion rules and incident cooperation. No sensitive contract/customer data, token or secret may appear in URLs, analytics, repositories or client-side code. Public information does not lose its public character, but compiled analyses, credentials and non-public client context remain protected.

## 10. Intellectual property and references

Pre-existing methods, templates, software, know-how and generic improvements remain with their owner. After full payment, the client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use contract-specific deliverables internally and for its own lawful bids/contracts. Raw reusable methods, third-party materials and source code are excluded unless expressly transferred. CONFENGE may not publish the client's name, logo, case, figures or testimonial without prior written authorization.

## 11. Compliance and public-sector integrity

Both parties must comply with applicable procurement, competition, anti-corruption and public-integrity rules. No payment covers success influence, facilitation, improper advantage, confidential public information or representation before a public agent. A credible integrity concern authorizes immediate preventive suspension and evidence preservation; confirmed illegality authorizes termination for cause, without prejudice to legal reporting duties.

## 12. Warranty, liability and force majeure

Each party is liable for direct, proven loss caused by its breach. Except for fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality/data-protection breach, intellectual-property infringement, corruption, unpaid fees and liabilities that cannot legally be limited, aggregate contractual liability is capped at the amount actually paid under the affected order during the preceding six months (or the one-off price, if higher). Neither party is liable for indirect, speculative or lost-opportunity damages to the extent permitted by law. Force majeure suspends the affected duty while the event and mitigation are documented; payment for already delivered services remains due.

## 13. Communications, changes, severability and forum

Operational notices use the registered email/channel; termination, default and legal notices require a durable written record. Material changes create a new `terms_version` and do not retroactively alter an accepted contract. If a clause is invalid, the remainder survives and the invalid clause is replaced by the closest lawful effect. Brazilian law governs. The elected forum is the district of CONFENGE's registered headquarters, except where a mandatory rule establishes another competent forum.
